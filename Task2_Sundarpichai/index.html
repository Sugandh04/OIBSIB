About Sundar Pichai

Sundar Pichai is an Indian-American business executive and the CEO of Alphabet and Google. He joined Google in 2004 and led the development of several widely used products including Google Chrome, Gmail, and Android.

His leadership style is known for calm decision-making, innovation, and empowering teams to build technologies used by billions of people worldwide.
