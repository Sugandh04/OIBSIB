About the CEO

Born in Madurai, India, Sundar Pichai's journey to the top of Silicon Valley is a testament to perseverance and brilliance. He joined Google in 2004, initially leading product management for Google's client software products.

Today, as the CEO of Alphabet and Google, he is steering the company through the complex new frontiers of artificial intelligence, quantum computing, and sustainable technology.
